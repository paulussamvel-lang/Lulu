## Five contracts

1. Extreme clarity: pale-blue rail, white task table, blue actions; fixed side navigation. First viewport shows task, engine, state, elapsed/remaining time and the next manuscript step. Signature: task state changes without losing the workflow rail. Familiarity is its tradeoff.
2. Warm editorial: ivory ground, vermilion actions, Songti manuscript text, sans controls; top navigation and paired manuscripts with a separate review column. Signature: selected sentences move into a sourced assembly. Long prose is its strength; scanning large queues is less dense.
3. Low-light studio: charcoal/slate, warm coral selection; persistent side navigation, large script pane, wave display and explicit voice settings. Signature: paragraph-to-voice handoff. It prioritizes audio composition and is less suited to bright ambient light.
4. Green archive: deep green navigation, pale-sage selection; source list, manuscript and excerpts remain alongside each other. Signature: switch source while preserving selection context. Excellent for growing libraries; three panes use more horizontal space.
5. Flow board: plum header over neutral lavender, four standard kanban columns. Signature: all source-to-voice stages at once, with the next action attached to each item. Good for multiple topics; single-document reading needs a detail view.
